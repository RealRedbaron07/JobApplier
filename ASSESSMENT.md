# Current Assessment

JobApplier is a local-first Flask/SQLite job-search operating system with deterministic parsing, explainable scoring, truthful drafting, tracking, follow-ups, contacts, analytics, and local backup/restore.

The earlier browser bot design is retired. Protected-site scraping, anti-detection behavior, portal login/form automation, automated messages, and application submission are outside the product boundary.

Known limitations are documented in [README.md](README.md), including deterministic parser coverage, the need for human fact checking, and the requirement to compile and inspect a final resume PDF outside the automatic draft flow.
