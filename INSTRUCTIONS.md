# Instructions

JobApplier now supports only local organization and application-package preparation:

1. Add jobs by form, pasted public text, CSV, or a JobApplier JSON backup.
2. Review the score, missing skills, authorization concerns, pay warnings, and deadline.
3. Generate resume and answer drafts at automation level 2 or higher.
4. Prepare a package at level 3 or higher.
5. Review every claim and complete the official application manually.
6. Explicitly confirm the tracker status after submission.

No legacy command may scrape a protected board, fill portal answers, or submit an application. See [USAGE_GUIDE.md](USAGE_GUIDE.md).
