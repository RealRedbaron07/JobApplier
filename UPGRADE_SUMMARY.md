# Upgrade Summary

The former browser-bot design has been replaced by the local-first, human-reviewed JobApplier OS.

Current supported capabilities include manual/pasted-text and owned-file ingestion, deterministic fit scoring, truthful LaTeX and answer drafts, application tracking, follow-ups, contacts, analytics, and CSV/JSON exports. Protected-board browser automation, login handling, anti-detection behavior, automated messages, and final submission are retired.

Use `python3 run.py`, then follow [README.md](README.md). Product boundaries are documented in [docs/ETHICAL_AUTOMATION_POLICY.md](docs/ETHICAL_AUTOMATION_POLICY.md).
